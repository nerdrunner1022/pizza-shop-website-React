 Vibe Coding Raw Mind Dump

## Core Vision & Concept
- This is a website for the Pizzeria named Antonio's Corner
- This website establishes an online presence for the physical storefront of the shop, allowing customers to get to know about the business, their menu and offerings and their availability.

## User Flow & Vibe
- The user has to be presented with the feeling of being in a pizzeria, with pizzas on display, and Italian colors and vibes used in the website.
- The website has to integrate the use of 3D Models, 3D Scenes, 3D effects and visuals, makeshift, or third-party 3D Models and 3D assets.
- The website uses a scroll-based storytelling model, instead of the traditional box-model layouts.
- The website will need a custom admin dashboard overlooking sales, orders, and other administrative tasks, accessed through the login menu in the public site
- These are the following core and essential sections and elements of the page; Hero, Interactive/Structured Menu, Location, Hours & Logistics, Online Orders, Reviews, The Heritage & Process Story regarding the craft, oven and heat used and Seasonal specials.

## Technical Notes & Constraints
- **Frontend Technologies**: React, Typescript, Tailwind CSS
- **Hosting**: Vercel
- **Repo Structure**: Monorepo, separate directories for the public site's frontend and the admin dashboard.
